## Review focus
- Manual to auto at a nonzero error must preserve initial output.
- Equation C must not show a proportional SP kick; A derivative kick must be filtered.
- Step estimates must not silently fit unsettled/no-response data.
- Demo/custom navigation must not leave animation callbacks active.
- Slow sweeps must retain reversals at differing refresh rates.
